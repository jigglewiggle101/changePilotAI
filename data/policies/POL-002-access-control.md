# POL-002: Access Control and Identity Changes
Status: Synthetic example policy for the ChangePilot AI portfolio.

## Requirements
Changes affecting identity records, employee entitlements, authentication or finance access require security review.
Validate both successful and denied access paths.
Use least privilege for service accounts and integrations.
Confirm audit events are emitted for entitlement changes and approval decisions.
Do not include credentials, tokens or personal data in prompts, logs or generated reports.

## Evidence
Record the relevant service IDs, test evidence, reviewer and unresolved exceptions.
