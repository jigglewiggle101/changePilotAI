# POL-004: Incident and Monitoring Readiness
Status: Synthetic example policy for the ChangePilot AI portfolio.

## Requirements
Identify active incidents related to affected services.
Define health indicators and alerting for the changed workflow.
Confirm logs and audit events are available to support investigation.
If an active high-severity incident affects a target or dependency, flag it for human review.
Do not infer causation from correlation alone.
