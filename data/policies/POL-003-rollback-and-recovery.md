# POL-003: Rollback and Recovery Readiness
Status: Synthetic example policy for the ChangePilot AI portfolio.

## Requirements
Changes to high-criticality services should identify a recovery strategy, rollback trigger, responsible team and verification steps.
Where rollback is not possible, document an alternative recovery plan and explicit risk acceptance.
A missing plan is a readiness gap, not proof that a change will fail.
Previous incidents should be considered as evidence but must not automatically be treated as proof of the same failure recurring.
